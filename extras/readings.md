# Great Readings in Heritage Studies

This curated collection brings together seminal monographs, transformative theoretical frameworks, and field-defining essays in Heritage Studies. Moving beyond introductory surveys, these works showcase how scholars and practitioners have challenged preservationist orthodoxies, historicized institutional power, and articulated the socio-political stakes of memory, curation, and conservation.
