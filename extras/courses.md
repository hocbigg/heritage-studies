# Great Courses

A curated selection of high-impact online courses, university lecture series, and specialized MOOCs in Heritage Studies. These courses offer audiovisual depth, field-specific case studies, and advanced institutional perspectives across material culture, built environments, provenance ethics, and digital safeguarding.

## Museums, Curation, & Material Culture

- [Tangible Things: Discovering History Through Artworks, Artifacts, Scientific Specimens, and the Stuff Around You (Harvard University / edX / Laurel Thatcher Ulrich)](https://www.edx.org/course/tangible-things-discovering-history-through-artworks-artifacts-scientific-specimens-and-the-stuff-around-you)
- [Arts and Heritage Management (Università Bocconi / Coursera / Andrea Rurale)](https://www.coursera.org/learn/arts-heritage)
- [Creating Meaningful and Inclusive Museum Practices (International Council of Museums / FutureLearn)](https://www.futurelearn.com/courses/inclusive-museum-practices)
- [Evaluation for Arts, Culture, and Heritage: Principles and Practice (University of Leeds & Centre for Cultural Value / FutureLearn)](https://www.futurelearn.com/courses/evaluation-for-arts-culture-and-heritage-principles-and-practice)

## Built Heritage, Architecture, & Archaeological Sites

- [Roman Architecture (Yale University / Open Yale Courses & Coursera / Diana E. E. Kleiner)](https://www.coursera.org/learn/roman-architecture)
- [Recovering the Humankind's Past and Saving the Universal Heritage (Sapienza University of Rome / Coursera / Paolo Matthiae & Davide Nadali)](https://www.coursera.org/learn/preserving-cultural-heritage)
- [Cultural Heritage and the City (European University Institute / FutureLearn)](https://www.futurelearn.com/courses/cultural-heritage-cities)
- [Cultural Heritage in Transformation (RWTH Aachen University / edX)](https://www.edx.org/learn/cultural-heritage/rwth-aachen-university-cultural-heritage-in-transformation)

## Heritage Crimes, Law, & Crisis Management

- [Creating a Digital Cultural Heritage Community (KU Leuven / edX / Fred Truyen & Sofie Taes)](https://www.edx.org/learn/digital-heritage/katholieke-universiteit-leuven-creating-a-digital-cultural-heritage-community)
