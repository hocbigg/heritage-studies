# Great Courses in Heritage Studies

A curated selection of high-impact online courses, university lecture series, and specialized MOOCs in Heritage Studies. These courses offer audiovisual depth, field-specific case studies, and advanced institutional perspectives across critical heritage theory, material culture, built environments, provenance ethics, and sustainable stewardship.

## Critical Heritage & Public History

- [What is heritage? (The Open University / OpenLearn)](https://www.open.edu/openlearn/history-the-arts/history/what-heritage/content-section-0)
- [Applied Public History: Places, People, Stories (University of London / Coursera / Catherine Clarke)](https://www.coursera.org/learn/applied-public-history)

## Museums, Curation, & Material Culture

- [Tangible Things: Discovering History Through Artworks, Artifacts, Scientific Specimens, and the Stuff Around You (Harvard University / edX / Laurel Thatcher Ulrich)](https://www.edx.org/learn/art-history/harvard-university-tangible-things-discovering-history-through-artworks-artifacts-scientific-specimens-and-the-stuff)
- [Arts and Heritage Management (Università Bocconi / Coursera / Andrea Rurale)](https://www.coursera.org/learn/arts-heritage)
- [Creating Meaningful and Inclusive Museum Practices (International Council of Museums / FutureLearn)](https://www.futurelearn.com/courses/inclusive-museum-practices)
- [Evaluation for Arts, Culture, and Heritage: Principles and Practice (University of Leeds & Centre for Cultural Value / FutureLearn)](https://www.futurelearn.com/courses/evaluation-for-arts-culture-and-heritage-principles-and-practice)
